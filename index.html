What these are. Ratios of a beat, rounded to whole milliseconds — the same numbers you'd get sketching a tempo map on paper. They don't know your room, your mics, your song, or the fact that the chorus lands two beats early. Every one of them is a starting point you're supposed to argue with.

What they aren't. Reverb decay here is expressed as a fraction of a beat, not a measured RT60 — RT60 belongs to a room, not to a tempo.
